# Phase 1 · Foundations (≈6 weeks)

**Goal:** get every intern to a common, solid baseline — comfortable with the tooling, the ML workflow, and honest evaluation — regardless of where they started.

> ⚙️ *Customize the week counts and specifics to match how you actually ran it.*

## What interns learned
- **Environment & version control:** Python, `git`/GitHub, virtual environments, reproducible setups. Every intern working in the open from week one.
- **The data stack:** NumPy, pandas, Matplotlib — fluency, not familiarity.
- **Core ML:** regression, classification, trees and ensembles (XGBoost/LightGBM), clustering, dimensionality reduction.
- **The workflow that matters:** train/val/test discipline, cross-validation, the bias–variance tradeoff, and **why the wrong metric lies to you** (accuracy vs precision/recall/F1/AUC).
- **Interpretability early:** SHAP and feature importance introduced from the start, not bolted on.

## Weekly shape (example)
| Week | Focus |
|------|-------|
| 1 | Tooling, git, environment, first notebook |
| 2 | NumPy/pandas fluency + EDA |
| 3 | Classical ML I — regression & classification |
| 4 | Classical ML II — ensembles & tuning |
| 5 | Evaluation, validation strategy, metrics |
| 6 | Interpretability + Phase 1 deliverable |

## Deliverable
A full scikit-learn pipeline on a real dataset — cleaned data, tuned model, honest evaluation, and a SHAP-based explanation — pushed to GitHub with a clear README.

## Assessed on
Correctness of the workflow, quality of evaluation, and clarity of write-up. See the [rubric](../evaluation/rubric.md).
