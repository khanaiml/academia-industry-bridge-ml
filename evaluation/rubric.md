# 📏 Evaluation Rubric

How interns were graded. Assessment was against explicit criteria, not impressions — the same dimensions that matter in both a research lab and an engineering team.

> ⚙️ *Adjust weights and wording to match how you actually assessed. The value here is that grading was structured and transparent.*

## Dimensions

| Dimension | What it measures | Weight |
|-----------|------------------|:------:|
| **Technical depth** | Correctness and sophistication of the ML work; understanding *why*, not just *how*. | 30% |
| **Reproducibility** | Can someone else run it and get the same result? Clean code, pinned deps, clear README. | 20% |
| **Rigor & honesty** | Sound baselines, proper evaluation, and honest reporting of limitations and failures. | 20% |
| **Communication** | Clarity of write-ups, figures, and code. Can they explain their work to others? | 15% |
| **Independence & ownership** | How much they drove the work themselves, especially in the research month. | 15% |

## Grade bands

| Grade | Bar |
|-------|-----|
| **Excellent** | Independently owned high-quality work across all phases; a research-month reproduction that genuinely understood and extended a paper; a deliverable that would stand up to outside scrutiny. |
| **Strong** | Solid, reproducible work across phases with minor gaps; clear communication; needed some guidance but delivered. |
| **Developing** | Completed the core work with support; gaps in rigor, reproducibility, or independence to close. |
| **Incomplete** | Did not meet the baseline deliverables for the phases. |

## Per-phase deliverables (recap)
- **Phase 1:** full scikit-learn pipeline with honest evaluation + interpretability.
- **Phase 2:** substantial end-to-end applied project with tracked experiments.
- **Phase 3:** paper reproduction + extension write-up with runnable code.
- **Phase 4:** containerized, deployed, monitored model with CI.

The **"Excellent" grade** is awarded only to interns who clear the top bar on every dimension.
